    Master Password Security

    Your master password is the foundation of your vault security. It should be:

    • Unique and strong (12+ characters recommended)
    • Always kept private and never shared
    • Never written down or stored openly
    • Changed periodically for enhanced security

    Encryption Information

    Your vault uses modern encryption standards:

    • Master Password: Argon2id key derivation
    • Stored Credentials: AES-256-GCM encryption
    • Authentication: HMAC-SHA256 verification
    • All operations: Zero-knowledge architecture

    Privacy Notice

    Effective: August 22, 2026

    SoyezSécurisé uses the information needed to create your account, protect access, and operate your encrypted vault.

    Information we handle

    • Your username, email address, account settings, and OTP configuration.
    • Encrypted vault entries, their labels, and any hints you choose to save.
    • Security and operational information needed to authenticate requests, maintain sessions, prevent abuse, and diagnose failures.

    How your vault is protected

    Your master password is processed in your browser to derive authentication and encryption keys. Vault secret values are encrypted on your device before they are sent to the service. Do not share your master password: we cannot recover it or decrypt your vault for you.

    How information is used

    We use account information to provide sign-in, OTP messages, encrypted storage, team features, account support, security monitoring, and service maintenance. Service providers may process limited information when they supply infrastructure or email delivery.

    Retention and your choices

    Account and vault information is retained while your account is active and as reasonably required for security, legal, and operational purposes. You can permanently delete your account and associated vault data from your profile. You may also contact SoyezSécurisé through the main website with a privacy request.
